How to play

  • You're a fluffy dog on the blue team. Pop the red team of cats with bubbles!
  • Attacking: carry the seed to site A or B and hold 4 to plant it.
  • Defending: stop them, or hold 4 next to their tree to uproot it.
  • Win rounds to earn doggy bank notes, then press B at the start of a round to shop.
  • First team to 13 rounds wins (5 in Swiftplay). Follow the floating labels if you're lost!
